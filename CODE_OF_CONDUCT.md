# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be kind. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- No publishing other people's secrets, keys, or private data.
- Disagree about code, not people.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers via GitHub. Maintainers may warn, restrict, or ban anyone who breaks this.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
